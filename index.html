About

I am a Mechanical Engineer (Management Trainee) at National Oilwell Varco (NOV FGS), with hands-on experience in equipment design, fabrication and technical analysis, supported by earlier process troubleshooting experience at PCA Technology.

My work involves SolidWorks modelling, steel fabrication, dimensional checks, equipment parameter studies and technical reporting. I work with operational teams to investigate issues, analyse data and develop practical recommendations.

My goal is to apply this technical foundation in oil and gas, supporting reliable equipment performance and practical problem solving in the field. I am interested in upstream operations, drilling and well services, including LWD/MWD.
